Montreal Canadiens History

The most storied franchise in NHL history.

Founded in 1909 in the Windsor Hotel as part of the NHA.

Key Players: Newsy Lalonde, Didier Pitre, Jack Laviolette

Stanley Cups: 1916

The Canadiens became an early NHL powerhouse. Overcoming challenges from key players Joe Hall(Spanish Flu - 37) and Georges Vezina(Tuberculosis - 39) Passing away.

Key Players: Georges Vezina, Newsy Lalonde, Howie Morenz, Aurel Joliat, Joe Malone

Stanley Cups: 1924, 1930, 1931

The Canadiens experienced a golden era during the 1940s and 1950s, winning multiple Stanley Cups and solidifying their legacy. Winning 5 straight Stanley Cups from 1956 to 1960. During the 1944-45 season, Maurice "Rocket" Richard became the first player to score 50 goals in a season, and on November 1, 1959, Jacques Plante became the first goaltender to regularly wear a mask, revolutionizing the position and improving player safety.

Key Players: Maurice "Rocket" Richard, Jean Beliveau, Doug Harvey, Bernie Geoffrion, Jacques Plante

Stanley Cups: 1944, 1946, 1953, 1956, 1957, 1958, 1959

The Canadiens continued to be a dominant force in the NHL during the 1960s and 1970s, winning 11 more Stanley Cups. Including 4 in a row. Goaltender Ken Dryden won the Stanley Cup and Playoff MVP awards before being considered a rookie, he had only played 6 nhl games. Jean Beliveau and Henri Richard won their 10th Stanley Cup together in 1971.

Key Players: Guy Lafleur, Ken Dryden, Larry Robinson, Bob Gainey, Henri Richard, Yvan Cournoyer

Stanley Cups: 1960, 1965, 1966, 1968, 1969, 1971, 1973, 1976, 1977, 1978, 1979

Post 1980 was a period of transition for the Canadiens, with the team experiencing some success but mostly challenges. Jean Beliveau won his league record 17th Stanley Cup as a member of the team's staff in 1993.

Key Players: Patrick Roy, Vincent Damphousse, Kirk Muller, Guy Carbonneau, Mats Naslund, Stephane Richer

Stanley Cups: 1986, 1993

In the 21st century, the Canadiens have faced ups and downs, with a focus on rebuilding and developing young talent. No cups wins and only one finals appearance in 2021. On March 12, 2019 Carey Price passed Jacques Plante for the most wins by a habs goaltender with 315

Key Players: Carey Price, P.K. Subban, Max Pacioretty, Brendan Gallagher, Shea Weber, Tomas Plekanec

Stanley Cups: None

The Canadiens are coming out of a rebuilding phase, focusing on developing young talent and building a competitive team for the future. They set the record for youngest average age team to make the playoffs in 2025.

Key Players: Cole Caufield, Nick Suzuki, Juraj Slafkovsky, Lane Hutson, Brendan Gallagher

Stanley Cups: None (yet)

Fun Facts About The Habs

Did you know these interesting facts about the Montreal Canadiens?

Stanley Cup Wins

The Montreal Canadiens have won the Stanley Cup 24 times, making them the most successful franchise in NHL history. First in 1916 and last in 1993.

Original Six

Although part of the Original Six, 8 others existed before, the Montreal Maroons, Wanderers, Ottawa Senators, Hamilton Tigers, Quebec Bulldogs, Pittsburgh Pirates, Brooklyn Americans, Philadelphia Quakers, and St. Louis Eagles.

Franchises First Goal

The Montreal Canadiens scored their franchise's first goal on December 19, 1909, against the Quebec Bulldogs. Scored by Joe Malone

Longest Winning Streak

The Canadiens had the worst record in the league in 1935-36 NHL season. Due to that, the NHL gave the Habs rights to all French Canadian players for two years.

Rocket Richard's 50 Goals

Maurice "Rocket" Richard was the first player in NHL history to score 50 goals in a season, achieving this feat in the 1944-45 season.

Jacques Plante's Mask

On November 1, 1959, Jacques Plante became the first goaltender to regularly wear a mask during NHL games, revolutionizing player safety.

Ken Dryden's Rookie Season

Ken Dryden won the Stanley Cup and Playoff MVP awards in his rookie season (1970-71), despite having only played 6 NHL games prior to the playoffs and not being considered a rookie yet.

Jean Beliveau's 10th Cup

Jean Beliveau won his 10th Stanley Cup in 1971, setting a record for the most cups won by a player with a single team.

Youngest Playoff Team

In 2025, the Canadiens set the record for the youngest average age team to make the playoffs in NHL history. And now broke it again in 2026.

Hall Of Famers

In the Hockey Hall of Fame, the Canadiens boast the second-most enshrined Hall-of-Famers with 42. All of their inductees are from Canada with the exception of former defenceman Joe Hall, who was from England.

Founding

The Canadiens were founded by J. Ambrose O’Brien on December 4, 1909 in the Windsor Hotel in Montreal as a charter member of the National Hockey Association.

Retired Numbers

The Canadiens have retired 13 numbers, by 14 players, in their history, the most of any team in the NHL, and the fourth highest total of any North American professional sports franchise.

GOAL!

The most team goals scored in a single game is 16 in a 16-3 win over Quebec in 1920.

Forget Something?

After winning the 1924 Cup, the Canadiens left the Cup on a curb when their car broke down, before driving back later to pick it up in the same spot they left it.

Champion City

6 Montreal teams have won the Cup including the Canadiens, AAAs, Victorias and Shamrocks. There has been 41 titles for the city.

Can't Lose

During the 1988-89 season, Patrick Roy went undefeated at home going 25-0-4.

Goalie Goal

On January 2, 2001 Jose Theodore scored the first goal by a Canadiens goalie.

Farewell Forum

On March 11, 1996 the Canadiens defeated the Dallas Stars 4-1 in the final game at the historic Montreal Forum after 72 years.

+/- Record

Larry Robinson owns the team record for best +/- in a season with +120 in 1976-77.

Winning Games

The longest undefeated(including ties) streak in team history is 28 games (23-0-5) from 12/18/1977 to 02/23/1978.

Playoffs

The Canadiens have won more cups than times they've missed the playoffs. 24 cups - 21 missed playoffs
