Montreal Canadiens History

The most storied franchise in NHL history.

Founded in 1909 in the Windsor Hotel as part of the NHA.

Key Players: Newsy Lalonde, Didier Pitre, Jack Laviolette

Stanley Cups: 1916

The Canadiens became an early NHL powerhouse. Overcoming callenges from key players Joe Hall(Spanish Flu - 37) and Georges Vezina(Tuberculosis - 39) Passing away.

Key Players: Georges Vezina, Newsy Lalonde, Howie Morenz, Aurel Joliat, Joe Malone

Stanley Cups: 1924, 1930, 1931

The Canadiens experienced a golden era during the 1940s and 1950s, winning multiple Stanley Cups and solidifying their legacy. Winning 5 straight Stanley Cups from 1956 to 1960. During the 1944-45 season, Maurice "Rocket" Richard became the first player to score 50 goals in a season, and on November 1, 1959, Jacques Plante became the first goaltender to regularly wear a mask, revolutionizing the position and improving player safety.

Key Players: Maurice "Rocket" Richard, Jean Beliveau, Doug Harvey, Bernie Geoffrion, Jacques Plante

Stanley Cups: 1944, 1946, 1953, 1956, 1957, 1958, 1959

The Canadiens continued to be a dominant force in the NHL during the 1960s and 1970s, winning 11 more Stanley Cups. Including 4 in a row. Goaltender Ken Dryden won the Stanley Cup and Playoff MVP awards beofre being considered a rookie, he had only played 6 nhl games. Jean Beliveau and Henri Richard won their 10th Stanley Cup together in 1971.

Key Players: Guy Lafleur, Ken Dryden, Larry Robinson, Bob Gainey, Henri Richard, Yvan Cournoyer

Stanley Cups: 1960, 1965, 1966, 1968, 1969, 1971, 1973, 1976, 1977, 1978, 1979

Post 1980 was a period of transition for the Canadiens, with the team experiencing some success but mostly challenges. Jean Beliveau won his league record 17th Stanley Cup as a member of the team's staff in 1993.

Key Players: Patrick Roy, Vincent Damphousse, Kirk Muller, Guy Carbonneau, Mats Naslund, Stephane Richer

Stanley Cups: 1986, 1993

In the 21st century, the Canadiens have faced ups and downs, with a focus on rebuilding and developing young talent. No cups wins and only one finals appearance in 2021. On March 12,2019 Cary Price passed Patrick Roy for the most wins by a habs goaltender with 315

Key Players: Carey Price, P.K. Subban, Max Pacioretty, Brendan Gallagher, Shea Weber, Tomas Plekanec

Stanley Cups: None

The Canadiens are coming out of a rebuilding phase, focusing on developing young talent and building a competitive team for the future. They set the record for youngest average age team to make the playoffs in 2025.

Key Players: Cole Caufield, Nick Suzuki, Juraj Slafkovsky, Lane Hutson, Brendan Gallagher

Stanley Cups: None (yet)
